The Oregon Trail

A rebuild of the 1985 game: you lead a wagon party along the trail to Oregon. Press Play. Keys: letters and digits as asked, Return, Backspace, the arrow keys and Space in the hunt, Control-S for sound.

Use my own files instead

If you have your own pictures (a folder with screens, images, font, hunt and optionally theme.json), tunes (MS0.BIN … TS.BIN) or the dialogue document, choose them here. They are read by this page and kept only in this browser; nothing is uploaded.
